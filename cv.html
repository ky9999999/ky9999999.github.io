CV

I am an associate professor at Tohoku University, Japan. Before taking my current position, I worked as an assistant professor at Tohoku University and as a contract researcher at the National Research Institute for Earth Science and Disaster Resilience (NIED) in Tsukuba, Japan, under Dr. Eiichi Fukuyama. I obtained my Ph.D. at Tohoku University under the supervision of Prof. Akira Hasegawa.
